D. Story — what to look for

  • 3–4 target words used with the right meaning and form (e.g. a belief as a noun; magical = has magic, not just “pretty”).
  • although joins two contrasting ideas.
  • Clear beginning and ending; a funny or surprising idea.

Model: Although the forest was scary at night, Nok went in to collect mushrooms for dinner. She found a magical mushroom that sang loudly whenever she tried to eat it. Now Nok never eats dinner — she just has very loud concerts.
